Wayfarer Support

Help with steps, weather, Stats, and your local Wayfarer data.

Steps are missing

Open Wayfarer while your iPhone is unlocked, then use its refresh control. Review Wayfarer’s Health permissions in iOS Settings and confirm Step Count and Walking + Running Distance access is enabled. Apple protects Health data while the phone is locked, so background updates can arrive later.

Weather is unavailable

Weather is optional. Check that Wayfarer has When In Use location permission and that the device is online, then try again from Today. Your steps and saved walking history remain available when weather cannot load.

Changing distance units

Open Wayfarer’s Settings tab and choose Automatic, Kilometres, or Miles.

Resetting local data

Deleting Wayfarer removes its locally stored preferences, cached history, and journey progress. It does not remove your source data from Apple Health.
