Wayfarer Support

Help with steps, Stats, artwork, weather, and your local Wayfarer data.

Steps are missing

Open Wayfarer while your iPhone is unlocked, then pull down on Today to refresh. In the Health app, open your profile, choose Apps, then Wayfarer, and confirm Step Count is turned on; Walking + Running Distance and Flights Climbed add distance and stairs. Wayfarer reads these types but does not write to Apple Health. Apple protects Health data while the phone is locked, so Wayfarer refreshes whenever you open it.

Looking back at a day or a month

In Stats, choose Steps, Distance or Stairs from the heading, then Week, Month or Year. Use ‹ and › to move back and forward in time. Tap a bar or a day to read it, then Open to see that day’s steps, distance, stairs and the hours you walked.

Weather is unavailable

Local weather is optional and provided through Apple Weather using WeatherKit. Check that Wayfarer has When In Use location permission and that the device is online, then try again from Today. Weather conditions around you help select the painting shown on Today. Wayfarer does not display air quality or provide a walking recommendation. If weather cannot update, your steps, artwork, and saved walking history remain available.

Moving around

Wayfarer has three spaces on one painting. Swipe right for Stats and left for the Collection; swipe up and down on Today for the next painting in today’s series. The dots at the bottom do the same by tap, and the button at the bottom right opens Settings. Settings → Swipe guide shows the swipes again.

Choosing a walk

Today shows one painting a day. Swipe left for the Collection: places from five famous walks (the Kumano Kodo, the Camino de Santiago, the West Highland Way, the Appalachian Trail and the Milford Track) and artwork by painters such as Monet and Hiroshige. Places are yours from the start; artwork unlocks with the steps you walk. Choose there what Today shows. Reopening Wayfarer always returns to today’s painting. Tap the name under the painting to read about it on Wikipedia.

Moving paintings

In places, water and waterfalls move, rain and snow fall, and lamps glow; everything else stays as painted. Artwork moves by its own light. Only the painting you are looking at moves. Turn on Reduce Motion or Low Power Mode to keep every painting still.

Artwork sources

Open Settings, choose Artwork, then Sources & attributions to view the source links.

Paintings not loading

Wayfarer downloads sharper paintings and new paintings from its artwork store. Until one arrives, it shows an included painting. In Low Data Mode, tap Download now on Today to fetch today’s painting. If a download fails, tap Try again.

Collection and steps

Places are yours from the start. Artwork unlocks one painting at a time with steps: you start with 60,000, and the steps you walk are added from Apple Health. Steps can’t be bought. Your unlocked paintings are kept in your own iCloud, so a new iPhone or a reinstall keeps them.

Privacy

Wayfarer has no account, advertising, or tracking. It sends a few anonymous usage events through TelemetryDeck that never include Health data or location. See the privacy policy.

Units

Wayfarer shows distance in kilometres and temperature in Celsius.

Resetting local data

Deleting Wayfarer removes its locally stored preferences and cached history. It does not remove your source data from Apple Health.
