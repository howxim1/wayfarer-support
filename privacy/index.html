Wayfarer Privacy Policy

Effective for Wayfarer 1.4 build 67, 9 September 2026

Wayfarer is designed to keep your walking experience on your iPhone. It has no account, advertising, developer analytics, tracking, or developer-operated backend.

Apple Health

With your permission, Wayfarer reads Step Count and Walking + Running Distance from Apple Health to show daily movement, walking history, comparisons, and journey progress. Wayfarer does not write to Apple Health. Health-derived values remain in Wayfarer’s local storage and are not sent to the developer.

Optional weather and location

If you choose Add weather, Wayfarer requests When In Use location permission. It supplies a foreground location through Apple’s WeatherKit service for each weather refresh. Current conditions around you—not conditions on the featured walk—help select the artwork variation shown on Today. Wayfarer does not retain those coordinates or send them to the developer.

Wayfarer does not request or display air quality, provide a walking recommendation, or send a location to an air-quality provider.

Scenic walks, artwork, and facts

Wayfarer’s scenic-walk catalogue, artwork, facts, and source information are included with the app. They do not require a content download or a developer server. Source links are available in Settings; a source website receives a request only if you choose to open its link.

Local data

Wayfarer stores preferences, cached walking history, journey progress, and scenic-content rotation state locally on your device. It does not use this information for advertising or tracking and does not sell it.

Deleting data and changing permissions

Deleting Wayfarer removes its locally stored app data. It does not delete information from Apple Health. You can review or revoke Health and location permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

Wayfarer is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If Wayfarer’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the changed version is distributed.
