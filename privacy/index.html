Wayfarer Privacy Policy

Effective 3 September 2026

Wayfarer is designed to keep your walking experience on your iPhone. It has no account, advertising, developer analytics, or developer-operated backend.

Apple Health

With your permission, Wayfarer reads Step Count and Walking + Running Distance from Apple Health to show daily movement, walking history, comparisons, and journey progress. Wayfarer does not write to Apple Health. Health-derived values remain in Wayfarer’s local storage and are not sent to the developer.

Optional weather and location

Weather is optional. If you choose Add weather, Wayfarer requests When In Use location permission and obtains one foreground location fix to request current conditions from Apple Weather. Wayfarer does not retain your coordinates or send them to the developer. Apple processes the weather request under its own privacy terms.

Local data

Wayfarer stores preferences, cached walking history, and journey progress locally on your device. It does not use this information for advertising or tracking and does not sell it.

Deleting data and changing permissions

Deleting Wayfarer removes its locally stored app data. It does not delete information from Apple Health. You can review or revoke Health and location permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

Wayfarer is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If Wayfarer’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the changed version is distributed.
